\section{Introduction}

Routing between a cheap adaptive predictor and a costly, potentially fallible
teacher predictor involves two potential benefits of escalation: improved
prediction for the current observation and information that can improve the
cheap predictor through adaptation. The present action can therefore affect
the cheap predictor's future competence.

The provisional scientific question is: \emph{Given a cheap adaptive predictor
and an expensive teacher predictor, how should a system decide whether to
route before the final operational response, replacing the cheap prediction
with the costly prediction, when that same query can improve the current
operational prediction and provide information that updates the cheap
predictor, changing future predictive and routing cost?}

The targeted selective-sampling and noisy-expert audit is complete, without
establishing novelty. Broad literature search is paused. The next step is to
define the exact operational and feedback protocol before developing a
mathematical model or proceeding to algorithms and experiments.

The dual use of a teacher query for the current task and subsequent learning
is not itself a novelty claim. The unresolved question is whether the routing
decision explicitly values the future effect caused by that learner update.
The feedback protocol remains open, and teacher-induced updates may harm the
cheap predictor; their future value is not assumed nonnegative.

Expected future learning value, fallible supervision, mistake--query
trade-offs, online updates, and multiple locally competent teachers have
prior precedents. Selective sampling must not be characterized globally as
myopic. The candidate conjunction requires routing before the final response,
operational replacement of F by D, updating F with that same response,
valuation of the immediate predictive consequence, explicit valuation of the
continuation-value change caused by that particular update, and allowance
for fallible D and potentially negative adaptation value. No direct equivalent
has yet been identified after targeted audit satisfying all six properties
jointly; this is not a proof of novelty.
